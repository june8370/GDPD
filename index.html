WHO WE ARE

A public news division.

Global Defence Press Directorate (GDPD) is a public-facing news division, focused on making defence, security and international affairs easier to follow. The site gathers reporting from established publishers and public sources, helping readers discover original coverage in one place.

Our editorial principle: inform the public, attribute sources clearly, and distinguish published reporting from verified fact or official statements.
